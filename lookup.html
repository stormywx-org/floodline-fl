Florida flood lookup

Type an address, town or ZIP code to see what our flood map shows at that spot. If you are in danger, call 911.

You can also tap the map. Your search goes to a map search service (Esri or OpenStreetMap) to find the spot; SWRF does not save it.

Results

Search for a place, or tap the map.

These are modeled estimates from river gauges, rain and land height, not inspections. Our map pixels are about 50 m (160 ft) wide, so a single house can be off. "6 ft or more" can be the river or a lake itself. Rain ponding is a screening estimate. Updated Fri Nov 20, 2026, 10:41 PM ET.
