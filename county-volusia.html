The St. Johns above Lake Harney (Geneva) is in major flood at 12.82 ft (Sun 7:30 AM ET), at or above its record of 12.67 ft (2022); NWS forecasts a crest of 13 ft (major) Sun Nov 22 around 1 PM ET. The St. Johns near DeLand is in moderate flood at 5.26 ft (Sun 7:30 AM ET); NWS forecasts a crest of 6 ft (major) Wed Nov 25 around 7 PM ET. The St. Johns at Astor is in major flood at 4.12 ft (Sun 7:30 AM ET); NWS forecasts a crest of 4.3 ft (major) Thu Nov 26 around 1 PM ET. River water is modeled along the St. Johns from Lake Harney to DeLand and Lake Woodruff. Heavy rain in the east (up to about 15 inches in 72 hours by radar estimate) drives the rain-ponding estimate there. The county is under a local state of emergency.
