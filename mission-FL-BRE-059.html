Mission FL-BRE-059 Low Open

Cocoa Beach, Brevard County. As of Sun Nov 22, 8:30 AM ET (Update 11/22 8:30 AM). 1 buildings at the forecast crest, 1 already 1 ft or more now, 0 are 18 in or more at crest. Deepest class: 1 ft to under 18 in. FEMA: 0 in the 1% floodplain, 0 in the 0.2% zone, 1 outside. Cell about 0.25 square miles.

Download detail map (PDF) Google Maps directions to the start point Volunteer Sign In / Out All missions

Safety. Do not enter floodwater. Floodwater can hide holes, currents and contamination. Eighteen inches or more of water means no vehicle access; go on foot only if it is safe, or skip that house and report it. Check in and out with your team lead. If anyone is in danger, call 911.

Start point

Begin at 28.29640, -80.60891 (building #1, near the center of the cell). Google Maps directions.

What to do

  1. Check in with your team lead before you start.
  2. Wellness check at each numbered building (is anyone home, is everyone all right).
  3. Note needs (food, medical, pump-out, shelter, no access) in the Notes column.
  4. Mark the building Visited in the Checked column.
  5. Check out with your team lead when you finish or if you have to stop.

Your team lead assigns this mission. Please do not start it until it is assigned to you. These pages do not list staff names or phone numbers.

Address list

How to report: tap an address, confirm the address shown on the form, fill in what you saw, add photos, then submit. If Google offers to continue an old draft, choose Start over. Sign in or out at the start and end of your shift: Volunteer Sign In / Out.

Field reports: tap the address of the house you checked (in this list or on the map) to open its report form. The mission, Map ID and address are filled in for you. Send one report per house, with water depth, damage, a severity from 1 to 5 and photos (photo upload needs a Google sign-in).

Depth is a modeled estimate in feet at the building footprint (Now = current gauge level or current ponding; Crest = NWS forecast crest, the lower of the last two tidal highs on the tide-affected river below Palatka, or the ponding depth for rain-only buildings). FEMA zone is from the official NFHL. Verify on the ground. The Checked and Notes columns are for use in the printed PDF.
