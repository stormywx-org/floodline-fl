Overview

Volunteer with SWRF

Stormy Weather Response Force (SWRF) is looking for volunteers to check on homes along the St. Johns River flood in Florida. Sign up below and a SWRF coordinator will assign you missions near you.

What volunteers do

  • Sign up so a coordinator can assign you missions near you.
  • Work a mission cell (about one-quarter square mile) with your team: check the listed homes, record water depth and damage, and send a field report with photos.
  • Sign in when you start a shift and sign out when you finish, so SWRF knows who is in the field and that everyone got home safe.
  • Stay in touch with your team lead. Please do not start a mission until it has been assigned to you.

Safety. Never drive or walk into floodwater. Floodwater can hide holes, currents and contamination. Eighteen inches or more of water means no vehicle access; go on foot only if it is safe, or skip that house and report it. If anyone is in danger, call 911. Volunteers must be at least 18 and agree to the SWRF volunteer waiver on the form below.
