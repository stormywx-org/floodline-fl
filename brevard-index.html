What it is

After the flood on November 19, 2026, Stormy Weather Response Force (SWRF) made a flood map. It shows which homes and businesses the water likely reached, and about how deep the water got.

Tell us your address. We will look it up on our map and follow up with you.

How it works

  1. 1Fill out the formGive us your name, how to reach you, and your address. It only takes a few minutes.
  2. 2We check our flood mapWe match your address to our map of where the water likely went.
  3. 3A volunteer reviews itA real person looks at your request and the map.
  4. 4We connect you with helpWe reach out and point you to help and resources near you.

Good to know

Our results are estimates. They come from river gauge readings and land height data. Nobody has inspected your building, so the real water level may be different.

Your information stays private. We use it only to help you.
