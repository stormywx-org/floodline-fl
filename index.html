How we estimate

  • River: we take live river levels from USGS gauges along the St. Johns, slope the water between them, and compare it with the ground height under every building. A building counts when the water reaches 1 ft or more at the building.
  • Rain: a screening estimate. We find low spots on the land, fill them with the rain that fell (radar estimate) minus what the drains are built to carry, and count buildings sitting in 1 ft or more of ponded water.
  • Reported areas: buildings inside neighborhoods where officials, NWS or the news reported flooding. Not all of them flooded.

Full methods, limits and sources

Good to know: these are estimates. They can be off in both directions. Not modeled yet: the tidal St. Johns below Palatka, smaller creeks, and Black Creek in Clay County.
